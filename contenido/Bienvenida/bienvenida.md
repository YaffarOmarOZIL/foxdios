## Tips

- **Double-click** icons to open them, just like on a real computer.
- Drag windows by their title bar, resize them from the edges, and drag one to the top of the screen to maximize it.
- Use the **3D window switcher** button next to the green Start button to flip through your open windows.
- On a phone? Tap **Mobile mode** on the desktop for a touch-friendly layout.
- Switch between **English and Spanish** with the **EN/ES** button on the taskbar.
